# Brand name options

**Decision: Kinpay** (chosen by the author). See the fit check at the end of this file. All project documents use this name.

Rules applied: no reference-app name, short (4-7 letters), easy to say in English and Hindi, and the idea fits shared money between friends.

## Shortlist

| Name | Idea | Why it works | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quits** | "We're quits": nobody owes anybody | Describes the end state the product creates. Short, friendly, a clear tagline: "Get to quits." | Common word, so the .com is likely taken. Try getquits or quits.app |
| **Evenly** | Splitting things evenly | Plain meaning, calm tone, good for trust | Long-ish. Likely used by small products already |
| **Dutch** | "Going Dutch": everyone pays their share | Very short, strong brand voice, easy logo | Wordplay could read as a nationality. Check it works for your audience |
| **Halve** | To split in two | Distinctive, easy to remember | Suggests only two-way splits |
| **Fairly** | Fair shares | Trust and fairness fit money | Generic. Crowded name space |
| **Tabsy** | A bar tab shared among friends | Playful, memorable | Close to existing "Tab"-style payment brands |
| **Kinpay** | Kin (family, friends) + pay | Warm, suits India's family and friends payments | Feels like a payments utility, less playful |
| **Chipin** | Everyone chips in | Clear action | Close to existing crowdfunding names |
| **Sorted** | Money matters, sorted | Positive, conversational | Very common word |
| **Potluck** | Everyone brings something | Fun, human | Less obvious as a payments brand |

## My recommendation

1. **Quits** for the strongest idea and tagline.
2. **Dutch** if you want the shortest and boldest option.
3. **Evenly** if you want the safest, most trustworthy tone.

## Before you commit

I have not searched for trademarks, app store names or domains, so none of these is confirmed free. For a portfolio piece the risk is small, but do these checks:

- Search the app stores and Google for the exact name plus "app" or "payments".
- Check .com, .app and social handles.
- Avoid names of known payment and finance brands, even if similar.

## Fit check: Kinpay

**Brand idea:** money that moves fairly and kindly between people who know each other, from one-off payments (v1) to shared group money (v2).

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the name say what the product is? | Yes. "Kin" means close people, "pay" says money. Easy to read and spell |
| Does it match the tone? | Yes. Warm and human, not corporate. Fits the polite wording rules in the PRDs |
| Does it work across both versions? | Yes. It suits v1 (pay a friend) and v2 (friends and groups) |
| Does it work in India? | Yes. Short, easy to say in English, Hindi and Gujarati. Family and friends payments are common |
| Weak points | "Kin" leans towards family, while the product also serves roommates and colleagues. The word "pay" makes it sound like a utility, and the fairness and splitting idea is not in the name |
| Fix for the weak points | Use a tagline that carries the split idea: "Money, kept fair between kin" or "Fair between friends" |

**Availability check (21 Nov 2026, light check only, not legal advice):**

- A web search found no known payments company called Kinpay. Similar names exist (KPay, KingsPay) in other markets.
- Domains: kinpay.com, kinpay.app, kinpay.io and kinpay.co already have DNS records, so they are probably registered or parked. kinpay.in and usekinpay.com showed no records, so they may be free.
- Not checked: trademark registers and app store names. Check these before any real use.

**Verdict:** a good fit, with the tagline as the fix for the one real weakness.
