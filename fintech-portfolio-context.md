### Rules for concept projects

- Title each one "Unsolicited redesign concept" and state I am not affiliated with the company.
- Use official logos from press/brand kits only. Do not present concept work as client work.
- Use realistic but made-up sample data (Indian names, ₹ amounts where relevant).

## Case study structure (for each project)

1. Problem: what is broken and who it hurts
2. Research: talk to 5 people, read 1 to 3 star app store reviews, read help center articles
3. Competitor analysis
4. User flows, including edge cases (failed, pending, offline, partial payments)
5. Final designs with a small design system
6. Clickable Figma prototype
7. What I would measure (completion rate, fewer support tickets, etc.)

Fintech design topics to show: onboarding and KYC, trust and clarity with money and fees, transaction states (pending, failed, reversed, refunded), error handling, security UX (OTP, biometrics), compliance and disclaimers.

## Finance basics per brand

### Revolut (neobank, UK)
- Bank in an app: account, card, transfers, currency exchange, savings, investing.
- Revenue: card interchange fees, interest on deposits, paid subscription plans, FX fees.
- 2025 results: revenue $6.0bn (+46%), pre-tax profit $2.3bn, net profit $1.7bn, 68.3M users.
- Key concepts for my project: P2P transfer, request money, balance vs pending, multi-currency, settle up.

### Ramp (spend management, USA)
- Company cards plus expense tracking, receipts, approvals, spend limits, accounting sync.
- Revenue: mostly interchange from card spend (so base software is free), plus paid software tier.
- Annualized revenue above $2B in 2026; valuation $60B (Oct 2026).
- Key concepts: expense, reimbursement, receipt / GST invoice, spend policy, approval workflow, category / GL code, month-end close, 3 roles (employee, manager, finance admin).

### Airwallex (cross-border business payments, Australia/Singapore)
- Multi-currency business accounts, international transfers, FX, accepting global payments.
- About $1.3B revenue run-rate after 74% year-over-year growth.
- Key concepts: exchange rate, FX markup/fees, multi-currency balance, supplier payment, transfer tracking.

Simple difference: Ramp is the company's expense manager. Airwallex is the company's international bank account.
