## 1. Summary

Version 1 got people paying and requesting. Version 2 turns [Brand] into a **place where friends and groups manage money together**.

Headline changes:
- Payments live inside a **conversation** with each friend or group.
- Groups get **real bill splitting** (amount, percent, share) and **settle up** tools.
- **Smart settle** reduces the number of payments needed to clear a group.
- Clearer **privacy and safety** controls.

## 2. Why version 2

What version 1 taught us (concept assumptions to validate):

| Observation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| A link request has no context after it is sent | People forget it and chase by chat anyway |
| Equal split is wrong for many bills | Users fall back to a calculator |
| Reminders were manual and per person | Organisers carry the social cost |
| Balances were scattered across groups | Users could not tell what they owe in total |
| Moving to a chat app to talk about money | We lose the conversation and the context |

## 3. Goals and non-goals

### Goals (v2)
- Make every payment and request live in a thread with its note, status and history.
- Let a group split any bill in three ways and settle with fewer payments.
- Cut the time to clear a group's balances.
- Give users control over who can find, message and add them.

### Non-goals (v2)
- Receipt scanning and line-item splits.
- More than one payer per bill.
- Multi-currency groups.
- A cross-group balance overview (considered for v2.1, see the evolution document).
- Investing, cards, perks, plans. Out of scope for this PRD.

## 4. Users

| Persona | What changed since v1 |
|---|---|
| **Aarav, roommate** | Wants recurring shared bills and fewer reminders |
| **Meera, organiser** | Wants to split unevenly and see who has paid at a glance |
| **Kabir, occasional** | Wants to reply and react in the same place as the payment |
| **Isha, trip planner (new)** | Wants one group for a whole trip with a clear end |

## 5. Scope

### In scope
1. **Payments tab** as a list of conversations, replacing the contact list.
2. **Conversation thread** with payment bubbles, notes, GIFs, emoji reactions, text.
3. **Request and Send** shortcuts above the message box.
4. **Groups** with cover, label, currency, Smart settle.
5. **Split bill** with Amount, Percent and Share modes.
6. **Settle up** with Remind and Mark as paid.
7. **Member management** with rules for leaving and removal.
8. **Home "Owes you"** card.
9. **Privacy controls**: discoverability, messaging, groups, name and photo visibility.
10. Scheduled and blocked payments in one place.

### Carried from v1
Signup and verification, send, link requests, scheduling, history, support.

## 6. User stories

- As a user, I can open a friend and see every payment, request and message in one thread.
- As a user, I can swipe a contact to request or send right away.
- As a user, I can attach a note, a GIF or a reaction to a payment.
- As an organiser, I can create a group with a name, cover and currency.
- As an organiser, I can select a bill I paid and split it by amount, percent or share.
- As a member, I can see exactly what I owe and to whom.
- As an organiser, I can remind everyone who owes me with one action.
- As an organiser, I can mark a share as paid when someone paid in cash.
- As a member, I can turn on Smart settle so the group makes fewer payments.
- As a user, I can control who can add me to groups and whether others can message me.
- As a user, I can see on Home who owes me and remind them.

## 8. Key flows

1. **Pay inside a thread:** Payments → friend → Send → amount and note → review → confirm → bubble.
2. **Request with a GIF:** Payments → swipe → Request → amount → note or GIF → send → bubble with Cancel.
3. **Create a group and split:** Payments → New → Group → members → name, cover, currency → create → Add a bill → pick transaction → Share mode → split → bubble.
4. **Settle up:** Group → Settle up → select members → Remind or Mark as paid → confirmation.
5. **Leave a group:** Details → Leave group → blocked if open balance → settle first.

## 9. Edge cases and states

| Situation | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Shares do not add up | Disable "Split with group", show the difference |
| Payer is not the user | Out of scope in v2. Known gap |
| A member is not on [Brand] | They get a link. They show as "Invite sent" |
| Member ignores reminders | Organiser can mark as paid or contact support |
| Messaging disabled by a contact | Explain in the thread. Payments still work |
| Leave or remove with open balance | Blocked with explanation |
| Block a person in a shared group | Blocked until they leave the group |
| Payment declined or insufficient funds | Error sheet with retry and add-money action |
| Offline | Blocking message and retry |
| Duplicate request for the same bill | Warn before sending |
| Smart settle turned on after bills exist | Recompute and show a preview before applying |

## 10. Success metrics

| Metric | Target for 90 days after launch |
|---|---|
| Users who open a thread each week | 70% of active users |
| Payments initiated from a thread | 60% of all peer payments |
| Groups with at least one split bill | 50% of groups |
| Median days to a settled-up group | down 35% against v1 |
| Reminders sent per settled group | down 30% against v1 (fewer chases needed) |
| Requests paid within 3 days | 75% (v1: 60%) |
| Groups using Smart settle | 40% |
| Support contacts about "who owes what" | down 40% |

Targets are planning assumptions for the concept.

## 11. Compliance and risk

- Messaging adds moderation and abuse risk. Provide block, report and privacy defaults off for discoverability.
- Payment details must remain visible for fraud and legal checks even with encrypted messages. Say so in plain words.
- Smart settle changes who pays whom. Show a preview and keep an audit trail for each balance.
- "Mark as paid" is a trust action. Log who marked what and when.
- GIFs come from a third party. Filter content and respect privacy.

2. Should Smart settle be on by default for new groups?
3. How do we handle a group with several currencies, for trips abroad?
4. Do we show a group overview on Home when a user belongs to many groups?
